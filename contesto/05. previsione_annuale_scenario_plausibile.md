# The annual forecast: a plausible medium scenario

_Written on 2026-09-19. It is a reasoned forecast, not a figure. It is there to give an idea of the
order of magnitude of a "normal" year. Every assumption is stated and is to be replaced with real
data as soon as it is available._

References: `03. analisi_costi_e_sostenibilita.md` (the costs per phase),
`04. analisi_costi_caso_peggiore.md` (the worst case).

> **Note.** This document is **not** the worst case. The failures to convert are distributed along
> the gates in the way considered most realistic. The comparison with the worst case is in § 6.

---

## 1. The assumptions on the market and on the funnel

**The market.** Private individuals, small associations, hobbyists and Italian micro-businesses
with a small, concrete problem, such as trackers, archives, league tables or small management
tools, willing to spend 400 € once.

**Acquisition.** Organic: SEO, word of mouth, posts in groups and communities, no paid
advertising. In a "normal" year, not the first, **500 visits a month, 6,000 a year** are assumed.

**The main market risk.** No-code tools exist (Airtable, Glide, Notion, AppSheet), and more and
more people build small tools by themselves with AI. This lowers the willingness to pay and the
rate at which people start.

**The annual funnel**, with the exits at the various gates:

| Step | Rate | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Visits to the site | — | 6,000 |
| Start the pre-analysis | 3% | 180 |
| Complete it | 50% | 90 (90 give up halfway) |
| … of which with the self-prompt, at no AI cost | 30% | 27 |
| Pass the prevalidator (the scope) | 65% | ~58 |
| Accept the analysis → development | 70% | ~41 |
| **Accept the demo → pay** | **60%** | **~25** |

The conversion is about 28% on the pre-analyses completed and 60% on the demos produced.

## 2. Technical choices and unit costs

- **The "economical" model profile, medium case**: Haiku for the pre-analysis, the prevalidator and
  the analysis helper; Sonnet for the analysis, the development and the tests.
- **The pre-analysis handled with a summary**, that is, with a constant context.
- A pre-analysis given up on costs 40% of a complete one.
- Free compliance corrections after delivery: 10% of the development cost of every project sold.

The AI cost for a complete pipeline, the economical medium profile: the pre-analysis $0.89 · the
prevalidator $0.01 · the analysis $0.25 · the helper $0.09 · the development $7.27 · the α-test
$2.42.

An exchange rate of 0.86 €/$. A price of **400 € VAT included**. Payment commissions: ~1.5% +
0.25 €.

| − IRPEF + surcharges | −1,988 |
| **= The net in hand over the year** | **≈ 2,410** |

Tax notes (the profile: an employee with a gross salary of 54k, the ordinary regime, the flat-rate
regime ruled out). The additional income takes the overall taxable base above 50,000 €, so the rate
changes:
- on the part below 50k: ~44.2% (33% of IRPEF, ~8.7% for the reduction of the employment
  deduction, ~2.5% of surcharges);
- on the part above 50k: ~45.5% (43% of IRPEF + surcharges).

## 6. Sensitivity and comparison

| Scenario | Visits/year | Sales | AI costs | Profit before tax | **Net** | Hours | **€/h** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prudent (typical of the first year) | 2,400 | ~10 | 180 € | 1,316 € | **≈ 560 €** | ~77 | **≈ 7** |
| **Plausible medium** | **6,000** | **~25** | **450 €** | **5,787 €** | **≈ 2,410 €** | **~139** | **≈ 17** |
| Good | 12,000 | ~49 | 901 € | 13,239 € | **≈ 5,500 €** | ~242 | **≈ 23** |

**The comparison with the worst case** (§ 04: Opus everywhere, heavy phases, every failure to
convert after the demo). At the same number of sales (~25) there is a **loss of ≈ 1,700 € a year**,
because every sale closes at −1.83 € and the fixed costs are left to pay. The distance between the
plausible scenario and the worst case depends almost entirely on three choices: the Developer's
model, the weight of every development and the rate of refusal at the demo.

## 7. Readings

- **The bottleneck is acquisition, not the AI cost.** In the medium scenario the AI costs ~450 € a
  year, against 8,000 € of net revenue. The result depends above all on how many people arrive and
  start the pre-analysis.
- **The fixed cost weighs on the first year.** With ~10 sales, the 1,665 € of fixed costs eat more
  than half of the margin.
- **Tax weighs about as much as the profit.** With a 54k salary, ~0.42 is left of every euro of
  profit.
- **17 €/h net in the medium scenario** is a supplement to one's income, not an activity that
  stands on its own. To change the order of magnitude, more volume is needed, that is, an
  acquisition that works, or a price or a consumption tier that values the complex projects more.
- **The worst case remains the risk to keep watch on.** Budgets per phase, an economical model for
  the Developer and the real measurement of the rate of refusal at the demo on the pilot project
  are needed.
