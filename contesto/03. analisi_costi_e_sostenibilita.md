# Analysis of the AI costs and of economic sustainability

_Written on 2026-09-19. Every figure is an **estimate** based on stated assumptions. They are to be
checked with a real pilot project._

References: `contesto/02. contesto_aggiornato.md` (the business model and the pipeline),
`struttura/design/Sequence.drawio.pdf` (the flow).

---

## 1. The starting point: the metrics of the working session of 2026-09-19

The development session with Claude Code (the site's restyling, the shared CSS, the deployer, the
`webtools_anagraphics` subsystem, the documentation) served as the first sample of real
consumption. The data come from the session's log.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| The user's messages | ~40 |
| Calls to the model (Claude Opus 5) | 157 |
| Input not in the cache | 282 tokens |
| Writing to the cache (a 1 h TTL) | 267,801 tokens |
| Reading from the cache | 26,548,397 tokens |
| Output (the reasoning included) | 138,439 tokens |
| **The equivalent cost on the pay-as-you-go API** | **≈ $19.40** (with no cache it would have been ≈ $137) |

The user has an almost flat account, so this cost was not paid. The figure is only there as a
reference.

Observations:
- Almost all of the volume is **re-reading the context**: at every call the whole conversation is
  sent again, on average ~194k tokens, up to ~339k at the end.
- A fixed base of instructions and tools: ~33k tokens at every call.
- **The screenshots weigh little**: 11 images, ~27k tokens in all, ≈ $2 out of $19.
- The files read or printed in full and the documentation loaded into the context weigh a great
  deal more.
- In a single session the cost grows more than linearly with the length. The main lever: **one
  session per subject**.

## 2. Corrections that came up during the discussion

The first estimates held wrong assumptions, corrected by the user:

- **No ready-made base.** The project does not sell customisations of existing products: every
  webtool is born from the client's specification. The cost cannot be cut "by starting from a
  scaffold".
- **The driver does not absorb the AI's failures.** The driver validates and supervises; unloading
  the work onto them means paying a human being.
- **The pre-analysis does not last 20 turns.** A non-technical user needs a great many more
  exchanges to arrive at a specification: 80–150 turns are realistic.
- **The failure to convert does not happen only in the pre-analysis.** One can leave at every gate
  of the Sequence, and the refusal after the demo is the dearest: the development and the tests
  have already been paid for.
- **Break-even is calculated on the margin before tax.** Under the ordinary regime the fixed costs
  are deductible. A first estimate divided the fixed costs by the net after tax and overestimated
  the number of projects needed.

## 4. The pre-analysis: the cost of a complete chat per user

**Assumptions**:
- the system instructions: 3,000 tokens;
- the user's message: ~80 tokens;
- the AI's answer: ~400 tokens, the reasoning included.

**Two ways of handling the conversation**:
- **The whole history**: the whole conversation is sent again at every turn. The cost grows more
  than linearly.
- **A summary**: the instructions, the updated draft specification and the last ~6 turns are sent.
  The context stays constant.

**The cache**: "yes" if the user answers within 5 minutes; "no" if they are slow and the cache
expires.

| Case | Turns | Model | History, cache yes | History, cache no | Summary, cache yes | Summary, cache no |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A prepared user | 30 | Haiku 4.5 | $0.11 | $0.36 | $0.29 | $0.33 |
| | | Sonnet 5 | $0.22 | $0.72 | $0.57 | $0.66 |
| | | Opus 5 | $0.55 | $1.81 | $1.43 | $1.66 |
| An average user | 80 | Haiku 4.5 | $0.38 | $1.92 | $0.76 | $0.89 |
| | | Sonnet 5 | $0.77 | $3.85 | $1.52 | $1.77 |
| | | Opus 5 | $1.92 | $9.62 | $3.81 | $4.43 |
| Non-technical | 150 | Haiku 4.5 | $0.97 | $6.13 | $1.43 | $1.66 |
| | | Sonnet 5 | $1.94 | $12.25 | $2.86 | $3.32 |
| | | Opus 5 | $4.84 | $30.63 | $7.14 | $8.31 |

Observations:
- The worst case is the non-technical and slow user with the whole history: up to $31 with Opus.
- **With the summary the cost stays proportional to the turns**, whatever the user's speed.
- The heaviest factor is the model: between Haiku and Opus there is a factor of 5. To be checked
  whether Haiku or Sonnet conduct a pre-analysis well.
- A 1-hour cache survives the long pauses but costs twice as much to write: to be weighed up.

## 7. Taxation (the profile: a Poste employee, a gross salary of 54,000 €)

The sources are at the bottom of the document. It is a rough estimate, not tax advice.

- **The flat-rate regime is ruled out.** Whoever in the previous year received income from
  employment above 35,000 € cannot use it; the threshold is confirmed for 2026 (L. 199/2025).
- **The ordinary regime**: the activity's income is added to the salary.
- **The IRPEF taxable base from employment** ≈ 49,000 € (the gross salary − the contributions borne
  by the employee, ~9.2%).
- **The 2026 IRPEF bands**: 23% up to 28k, **33%** from 28 to 50k (it was 35%), 43% above 50k.
- **The effective marginal rate ≈ 44%**:
  - 33% of IRPEF;
  - ~8.7% from the reduction of the employment deduction between 28 and 50k
    (1,910 € × (50,000 − R) / 22,000);
  - ~2.5% of regional and municipal surcharges (they vary by territory).
- **INPS Gestione Separata 24%** (the reduced rate for whoever is already covered by another
  compulsory pension scheme), if the activity is classified as self-employment.
- **VAT 22%**: the price of 400 € is meant as VAT included (the price to the consumer). If it were
  400 € + VAT, the net takings would be 400 €.

### 7.1 The net per project sold

The exchange rate assumed: 0.86 €/$. Payment commissions: Stripe, ~1.5% + 0.25 €.

| Item | Top unfavourable | Economical medium |
|---|---|---|
| The price to the client | 400.00 | 400.00 |
| − VAT 22% | −72.13 | −72.13 |
| = Takings net of VAT | 327.87 | 327.87 |
| − The AI cost | −152.95 ($177.85) | −21.42 ($24.91) |
| − Payment commissions | −6.25 | −6.25 |
| **= The margin before tax** | **168.67** | **300.20** |
| − INPS 24% | −40.48 | −72.05 |
| − IRPEF + surcharges (~44%) | −56.64 | −100.80 |
| **= The net in hand per project** | **≈ 71.55** | **≈ 127.35** |

### 7.2 The annual fixed costs

| Item | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| An accountant (the ordinary regime) | 800 € | 1,500 € |
| PEC + a digital signature | 50 € | 50 € |
| Hosting (a VPS for the backend and Mongo) | 120 € | 480 € |
| A domain | 15 € | 15 € |
| **Total** | **≈ 1,000 €** | **≈ 2,050 €** |

The driver's share is left out of this calculation.

### 7.3 Break-even: projects sold per year

Break-even comes when the margins before tax cover the fixed costs. At zero profit neither IRPEF
nor INPS is paid.

| Scenario | Fixed costs of 1,000 € | Fixed costs of 2,050 € |
|---|---|---|
| Top unfavourable | **6** | **13** |
| Economical medium | **4** | **7** |

**One project a month (12 a year) in the top unfavourable case**: margins ≈ 2,024 €.
- With fixed costs of 1,000 €: a profit of ≈ 1,024 € before tax, **≈ 430 € net** a year.
- With fixed costs of 2,050 €: **about break-even**.

## 8. Working time

- The time estimated by the user: **1.5 hours per project**.
- With 12 sales a year and a 20% conversion, **60 projects** enter the funnel → cautiously
  **120 hours a year, that is, 10 hours a month**.
- The estimate is generous: about 24 of the 60 projects are set aside by the prevalidator with no
  human intervention. The real work concerns about 36 analyses to validate and 24 developments to
  supervise, 12 with the demo accepted and 12 with it refused.
- Left out are the fixed time for the VAT number and the accountant and the maintenance of the
  pipeline and the servers.

**The hourly pay in the top unfavourable case with 12 sales a year**:
- ≈ 3.60 €/h net with fixed costs of 1,000 €;
- ≈ 0 €/h with fixed costs of 2,050 €.

**The conclusion for the worst scenario**: the shack stays afloat but one works almost for nothing.
The real earnings depend on two factors:
- AI costs close to the economical scenario: the margin per project goes from ~169 € to ~300 €;
- a higher conversion.

## 9. The levers and the risks identified

**Where the cost can explode**
- Development cycles with no limit: an agent that fails and tries again indefinitely. A maximum
  budget per phase and per project is needed, with an automatic stop.
- A context that swells: every phase must start clean and receive only what it needs.
- A pre-analysis with the whole history and slow users: use the summary.
- Refused demos: every refusal is a complete development not paid for.

**Cost levers**
- The choice of the model per phase: between Haiku and Opus there is a factor of 5.
- Prompt caching; the Batch API (−50%) for the non-interactive processing.
- The self-analysis prompt, run with the user's AI, costs nothing.

**Measurements to make with a pilot project** (in order of importance)
1. The **Developer**'s real consumption on a real webtool.
2. The **rate of refusal at the demo**.
3. The real number of turns of the pre-analysis with non-technical users.

**The connection with the consumption tier**: by recording the tokens of every call per project,
the *costs and revenues calculator* has real data. The custom price at the demo becomes "the
consumption measured + the driver's share".
